## ADHDoTECH Together

Do you have ADHD? Our mentoring program is specifically designed for people with ADHD who want to tackle the challenges of ADHD and turn them into practical tools for success in the high-tech world. In a program led by experienced mentors from the high-tech world, you will receive tools for time management, productivity, effective communication and teamwork. The program will help you strengthen your unique abilities, such as creativity, innovation and thinking outside the box.

Together, we will build an action plan tailored to your personal needs to succeed in various roles in high-tech and maximize your potential.

Ready to break through?

[Button: Mentoring Program](https://www.adhdotech.org/en/mentoring-program)
