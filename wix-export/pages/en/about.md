# Who we are – ADHDoTECH

##### Enabling ADHD workers to thrive in the high-tech industry

At ADHDoTECH, we are leading a real change in the approach to employing people with ADHD in the Israeli high-tech industry. We believe that ADHDer's talents are unique assets to organizations: groundbreaking creativity, relentless curiosity, mental toughness and empathy, mental flexibility, and the ability to function exceptionally in environments of uncertainty and pressure. These abilities place them at the forefront of Leading innovation – exactly what it takes to lead and succeed in the high-tech world.

Despite these benefits, challenges such as burnout, job instability, and misperception of their abilities may prevent them from realizing their full potential. We are here to bridge the gap between the individual's talent and challenges and lead to successful and meaningful integration into the High Tech industry.

## High-tech & ADHD – Winning combination

The high-tech industry requires creative, quick-thinking, and flexible workers, traits that characterize many people with ADHD.

ADHDoTECH works to uncover and maximize this potential, implementing solutions that enable employees and managers to thrive together.

## How we Do it ?

- Personal mentoring program:
  
  Personal and focused support for people with ADHD, from the beginning to successful integration into work.
- Training for managers:
  
  Practical workshops to train managers to use customized management tools, which will lead to the development and empowerment of employees with attention deficit disorders (ADHDoers) for the benefit of the team's and the organization's achievements.
- A professional and supportive community:
  
  A platform for sharing knowledge, consultation, and collaboration through meetups and lectures with experts in the field.

## Join us

Come be part of a revolution that is changing the world of work in Israel! We invite you – investors, managers, and potential partners – to leverage the unique talent of people with ADHD and create a diverse, innovative, and inspiring work environment.

Let's uncover the true potential together!
