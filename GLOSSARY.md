# ADHDoTECH Website

The public website of ADHDoTECH, an Israeli nonprofit unlocking ADHD talent in high-tech, in Hebrew and English.

## Site

**Stage 1**:
The first version of the site after leaving Wix: static pages carrying the old site's content, with no online payments, forms or logins.
_Avoid_: MVP, v1, launch

**Past Event**:
An event ADHDoTECH has already held (a lecture, workshop or meetup), shown on the site as a read-only listing with its date, description and recording.
_Avoid_: session, event page, webinar

**Members Area**:
The pages only ADHDoTECH members may read, including the pricing plans, opened with the Shared Password.
_Avoid_: member portal, private pages, login area

**Shared Password**:
The single password ADHDoTECH gives its members to open the Members Area; there are no individual member accounts.
_Avoid_: member login, credentials
