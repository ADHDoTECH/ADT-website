## Our unique Mentoring program for ADHDoers: The path to success in high-tech

If you are struggling with ADHD and are looking for tools to help you get the most out of your work in the high-tech world and in general, our mentoring program is the answer for you. This unique program is designed for those who want to turn personal challenges into professional advantages and place ADHD in a place of growth and development.

### Effectiveness measurement and analysis of results

The effectiveness of the program is measured through the program:

- Mapping questionnaires at the start and end of the program: assist in setting personal goals and measuring improvement along the way.
- Ongoing feedback: We conduct a continuous process of collecting feedback from participants for continuous improvement.

At the end of the program, a summary report will be shared with both the mentee and the mentor, and in cases of external funding – also to the program's clients, subject to the mentee's approval.

- 31% improvement in overall coping with the difficulties of the KSHAR
- 50% reduction in procrastination incidents

- 35% improvement in message delivery and communication

#### Mentees

#### What will you get in the program?

For approximately 12 weeks, you will undergo an in-depth and personalized session in which you will receive all the tools required for success at work.

Our mentoring program focuses on two key aspects:

1. Overcoming challenges: time management, dealing with procrastination, improving communication skills, anger management, and harnessing resources at work.
2. Understanding strengths: creativity, empathy, ability to function in situations of uncertainty, curiosity, and motivating others to action.

During the program, you will undergo a comprehensive personal mapping of your current situation, we will set personalized goals together, and we will help you implement the tools you learn to get the most out of your ADHD.

#### How does it work?

- 10 personal 1:1 sessions: One-hour meetings, face-to-face or Zoom meetings, to maximize flexibility and efficiency.
- Weekly follow-up calls: Short calls aimed at adapting the content to your personal needs and monitoring your progress.
- The mentoring program provides ongoing guidance and support that begins at the registration stage, including matching the most appropriate mentor for you.
- The program costs 1500 NIS, which is a donation to the NPO to support our social impact.

#### Mentors

### Our mentors – The force that drive success

The mentors in our program are high-tech professionals with personal experience with ADHD. They not only understand the challenges you experience but also know how to leverage the benefits of ADHD to succeed in the professional world.

#### What makes our mentors unique?

- Rigorous screening process: Mentors are selected after a rigorous screening process, which ensures that they are committed to fully supporting you.
- Personalization: We make a special effort to match the mentor who will best support you.
- Dedicated training: The mentors undergo unique training, which gives them the tools required to provide maximum assistance to those with ADHD.

The program is managed and supervised by professionals who themselves successfully deal with attention deficit disorder, and are familiar with the high-tech world.

### What does the ADHDoTECH mentoring program include?

The mentoring program provides support and personal guidance to ADHD employees and high-tech job seekers, with an emphasis on strengthening professional and managerial skills.

### How long does the mentoring program last?

The program lasts 12 weeks and consists of 10 sessions.

### How are the meetings in the mentoring program held?

The meetings are held in a combined format: some on Zoom and some face-to-face.

### How do you choose the mentor that is right for me?

The mentor is selected according to your needs, through a careful matching process, to ensure that you receive the best support.

### Is the mentoring program tailored to my personal needs?

Yes, the program is built according to the participant's personal needs and a unique work plan is built. During the program, weekly follow-up calls are held that are tailored to your personal needs.
