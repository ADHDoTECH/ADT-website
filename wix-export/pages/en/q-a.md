### What is the core value that drives ADHDoTECH?

We believe in harnessing the unique talents of people with ADHD and turning the challenges they experience into superpowers that create real value for organizations and employees.

### Who can benefit from ADHDoTECH services?

- Workers with ADHD who want to succeed in high-tech
- Employers who seek diversity and understand that 10% of high-tech workers have ADHD
- Organizations and startups looking for tools to effectively manage diverse teams

### What are the challenges that people with ADHD experience in the workplace?

Despite their talent, they may face burnout, frequent job transitions, inefficient task management, and a sense of professional instability, which hinders the fulfillment of their potential.

### What are the benefits of working with ADHD in the high-tech industry?

People with ADHD are characterized by abilities such as creativity, quick decision-making, mental flexibility, mental toughness, empathy, and the ability to function excellently in stressful and uncertain environments – critical traits in high-tech.

### Why is high-tech the ideal environment for people with ADHD?

High-tech requires decision-making under conditions of uncertainty, problem-solving on the fly, and creative thinking – traits that naturally characterize people with ADHD and allow them to thrive in the field.

### How are the meetings in the mentoring program held?

The meetings are held in a combined format: some on Zoom and some face-to-face.

### How do I choose the mentor that is right for me?

The mentor is selected according to your needs, through a careful matching process, to ensure that you receive the best support.

### What is the ADHDoTECH professional community?

The ADHDoTECH community offers a place for collaboration, learning, and open discussions with monthly lectures, meetups, and meetings with industry experts.

### How can I join or collaborate with ADHDoTECH?

You can join our community, participate in meetings, or join us as investors and partners to create a diverse, supportive and leading workplace in the high-tech field.
